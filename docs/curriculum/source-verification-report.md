## 1.1 MoEYS 2016 Curriculum Framework Study-Hours Analysis

The January 2016 MoEYS Curriculum Framework (pp. 24–26) outlines the overarching macro-structure for general education:

### Lower Secondary (Grades 7–9) Framework Table (40 Hours/Week Total):
1. **Khmer Language:** 7 hours/week
2. **Foreign Languages (English/French):** 6 hours/week
3. **Mathematics:** 7 hours/week
4. **Science (Integrated domain):** 6 hours/week *(includes Physics, Earth-Environmental Science, Chemistry, Biology)*
5. **Social Studies (Integrated domain):** 7 hours/week *(includes History, Geography, Moral-Civics, Home Economics)*
6. **Information and Communication Technology (ICT):** 2 hours/week
7. **Physical Education and Sports:** 2 hours/week
8. **Arts Education:** 1 hour/week
9. **Health Education:** 1 hour/week
10. **Local Life Skills:** 1 hour/week

> [!NOTE]
> **Implementation Note on Science & Social Studies**: While the framework defines Science and Social Studies as integrated subject areas with component disciplines, in actual Cambodian junior secondary school scheduling and textbook publishing, MoEYS issues distinct, dedicated textbooks and assigns specific weekly teaching periods for Physics, Chemistry, Biology, Earth & Environmental Science, History, Geography, and Moral-Civics. Furthermore, school timetables operate between 33 core periods (examinable core) and 40 periods (inclusive of non-examinable co-curricular subjects).

### Upper Secondary (Grades 10–12) Framework Table:
- **Grade 10:** Common diagnostic core (34 periods/week) establishing foundational competencies before academic tracking.
- **Grades 11–12 (Science Stream):** 36 hours/week focusing on Advanced Mathematics (6h), Physics (4h), Chemistry (4h), and Biology (4h).
- **Grades 11–12 (Social Science Stream):** 36 hours/week focusing on Khmer Literature (6h), History (4h), Geography (4h), and Moral-Civics (4h).

## 3. Distinction of Source Categories

ScienceKH enforces strict provenance tagging across all educational artifacts:

1. **[MOEYS-OFFICIAL-CURRICULUM]**: Statutorily approved national curriculum frameworks and syllabuses issued directly by MoEYS decree (*ប្រកាស*).
2. **[MOEYS-TEXTBOOK-PRIMARY]**: Core student textbooks compiled by the Department of Curriculum Development and published for nationwide distribution.
3. **[MOEYS-TEACHER-GUIDE]**: Methodological teacher guides (*សៀវភៅគ្រូ*) detailing pedagogical milestones, recommended class periods, and experimental protocols.
4. **[MOEYS-EXAM-ARCHIVE]**: Official past examination papers administered in nationwide Baccalaureate II (*បាក់ឌុប*) and Diploma (*ឌីប្លូម*) examinations, preserved with authenticated marking schemes.
5. **[SCIENCEKH-SUPPLEMENTARY]**: Didactic expansions created by ScienceKH subject-matter experts (worked derivations, multi-step problem explanations, conceptual checks, and interactive discovery simulations). These are always explicitly designated as supplementary to prevent confusion with official ministry exam questions.
