## 1. Khmer Language & Literature (ភាសាខ្មែរ ថ្នាក់ទី ៧)

### Subject Overview & Competencies:
Focuses on preserving classical Khmer poetic heritage, rigorous grammatical word classifications, reading comprehension of narrative texts, and foundational composition skills.

### Units & Lessons Breakdown:
#### Unit 1: កាព្យឃ្លោងខ្មែរ និងវេយ្យាករណ៍គ្រឹះ (Classical Poetic Meters & Grammar)
- **Lesson 1 (`les-g7-khmer-01`):** កាព្យឃ្លោងខ្មែរ (បទកាកគតិ និងបទពាក្យ ៧) (Khmer Poetic Meters)
  - *Kak-Kati Meter:* 7 phrases per stanza, 4 syllables per phrase (28 syllables total). Strict rhyme pattern: phrase 1 rhymes with phrase 2 (syllable 4 with syllable 4); phrase 2 rhymes with phrase 3 (syllable 4 with syllable 2); phrase 7 rhymes with phrase 2 of the next stanza.
  - *Pathya Vat (7-Syllable Meter):* 4 phrases per stanza, 7 syllables per phrase. Syllable 7 of line 1 rhymes with syllable 4 of line 2.
- **Lesson 2 (`les-g7-khmer-02`):** ថ្នាក់ពាក្យ នាម កិរិយា និងគុណនាម (Parts of Speech in Khmer)
  - *Word Classes:* នាម (Nouns: សិស្ស, ផ្ទះ), កិរិយា (Verbs: រៀន, ដើរ), គុណនាម (Adjectives: ឆ្លាត, ស្អាត). Syntax rule: in Khmer, adjectives strictly follow the noun they modify (e.g. *សិស្សឆ្លាត*).

## 2. History (ប្រវត្តិវិទ្យា ថ្នាក់ទី ៧)

### Subject Overview & Competencies:
Explores prehistoric archaeology on Cambodian soil and traces the formation of the first centralized maritime civilization in mainland Southeast Asia.

### Units & Lessons Breakdown:
#### Unit 1: បុរេប្រវត្តិសាស្ត្រកម្ពុជា និងការកកើតនគរភ្នំ (Prehistory & Rise of Funan)
- **Lesson 1 (`les-g7-hist-01`):** បុរេប្រវត្តិសាស្ត្រកម្ពុជា (ស្ថានីយល្អាងស្ពាន និងសំរោងសែន) (Cambodian Prehistory)
  - *Archaeological Evidence:* ស្ថានីយល្អាងស្ពាន (Battambang: Hoabinhian pebble tools dating to 7000 BCE); ស្ថានីយសំរោងសែន (Kampong Chhnang: polished stone adzes, bronze axes, cord-marked pottery).
- **Lesson 2 (`les-g7-hist-02`):** នគរភ្នំ (ហ្វូណន)៖ ការកកើត និងពាណិជ្ជកម្មកំពង់ផែអូរកែវ (Funan Kingdom & Oc Eo Trade)
  - *Origins:* Chinese records (Kang Tai & Zhu Ying) document the union of Preah Thong (Huntian) and Neang Neak (Liuye) establishing Funan (1st–6th century CE).
  - *Oc Eo Entrepôt:* Major international port linking Indian, Chinese, and Roman maritime merchants (yielding Roman coins and gold artifacts). Advanced canal excavation for drainage and boat transport.

## 3. Geography (ភូមិវិទ្យា ថ្នាក់ទី ៧)

### Subject Overview & Competencies:
Details Cambodia's physical landforms, river hydrology, the unique reverse-flow mechanism of Tonle Sap Lake, and the tropical monsoon climate cycle.

### Units & Lessons Breakdown:
#### Unit 1: ភូមិសាស្ត្ររូបវន្តកម្ពុជា ជួរភ្នំ និងប្រព័ន្ធទឹក (Physical Geography & Hydrology)
- **Lesson 1 (`les-g7-geo-01`):** ភូមិសាស្ត្ររូបវន្តកម្ពុជា (ទន្លេមេគង្គ និងបឹងទន្លេសាប) (Mekong & Tonle Sap System)
  - *Hydrology:* In the rainy season (June–Oct), swelling Mekong floodwaters force the Tonle Sap River to reverse its flow northward into Tonle Sap Lake, expanding the lake's surface from $2,700\text{ km}^2$ to over $16,000\text{ km}^2$. In the dry season, the flow reverses southward.
- **Lesson 2 (`les-g7-geo-02`):** សណ្ឋានដី និងអាកាសធាតុកម្ពុជា (Landforms & Monsoon Climate)
  - *Physiographic Regions:* 1. Central alluvial plain; 2. Surrounding mountains (Cardamom with Mt. Aural at 1,813m; Dangrek Escarpment); 3. Coastal strip (440 km).
  - *Monsoons:* Southwest monsoon (May–Oct: wet season) vs Northeast monsoon (Nov–Apr: dry season).

## 4. English as a Foreign Language (ភាសាអង់គ្លេស ថ្នាក់ទី ៧)

### Subject Overview & Competencies:
Follows the official MoEYS *English for Cambodia - Book 7*, building everyday communicative competence, regular and irregular past tense narration, and classroom discourse.

### Units & Lessons Breakdown:
#### Unit 1: Present & Past Tenses in Daily Life
- **Lesson 1 (`les-g7-eng-01`):** Present Simple & Everyday Routines
  - *Grammar:* Form: *Subject + Verb(s/es)*. Habitual actions, frequency adverbs (*always, usually, sometimes, never*).
  - *Example:* "Dara studies mathematics every morning at 7:00 AM."
- **Lesson 2 (`les-g7-eng-02`):** Past Simple Tense: School Days & Personal Narratives
  - *Grammar:* Regular verbs (+ed: *played, visited*); high-frequency irregulars (*went, saw, wrote, had*). Negative form: *did not + base verb*. Questions: *Did + subject + base verb?*
  - *Example:* "We visited the National Museum in Phnom Penh last week."

## 5. ICT & Computing (ព័ត៌មានវិទ្យា ថ្នាក់ទី ៧)

### Subject Overview & Competencies:
Develops digital literacy: computer hardware architecture, operating system file management, and proficient typing using the national Khmer Unicode standard.

### Units & Lessons Breakdown:
#### Unit 1: ផ្នែករឹងកុំព្យូទ័រ និងការវាយអក្សរខ្មែរយូនីកូដ (Hardware & Khmer Unicode)
- **Lesson 1 (`les-g7-ict-01`):** ផ្នែករឹងកុំព្យូទ័រ និងស្ថាបត្យកម្មប្រព័ន្ធ (Computer Hardware Architecture)
  - *Components:* CPU (Central Processing Unit - ខួរក្បាលកុំព្យូទ័រ), RAM (Random Access Memory - អង្គចងចាំបណ្តោះអាសន្ន), Storage (SSD/HDD - ឧបករណ៍ផ្ទុកទិន្នន័យអចិន្ត្រៃយ៍), Input/Output devices.
- **Lesson 2 (`les-g7-ict-02`):** ការវាយអក្សរខ្មែរយូនីកូដ និងការរៀបចំឯកសារ (Khmer Unicode & Word Processing)
  - *Typing Rules:* Using the **\`j\` (Coeng)** key to generate subscript consonants (e.g. \`k\` + \`j\` + \`n\` = ក្ន). Standard MoEYS fonts (Khmer OS Battambang, 12pt body, 1.15 line spacing).

## 6. Moral & Civics (សីលធម៌ និងពលរដ្ឋវិជ្ជា ថ្នាក់ទី ៧)

### Subject Overview & Competencies:
Instills family values, filial piety, mutual respect, understanding of the UN Convention on the Rights of the Child, and active student citizenship in schools.

### Units & Lessons Breakdown:
#### Unit 1: សីលធម៌ក្នុងគ្រួសារ និងសិទ្ធិកុមារ (Family Ethics & Child Rights)
- **Lesson 1 (`les-g7-moral-01`):** សីលធម៌ក្នុងគ្រួសារ និងសាលារៀន (Family & School Ethics)
  - *Values:* Filial piety (*កតញ្ញូតាធម៌* — gratitude towards parents), respect for teachers, caring for elderly family members, and observing school regulations.
- **Lesson 2 (`les-g7-moral-02`):** សិទ្ធិកុមារ និងកាតព្វកិច្ចពលរដ្ឋក្នុងសាលារៀន (Child Rights & Civic Duties)
  - *UNCRC 4 Pillars:* 1. សិទ្ធិរស់រានមានជីវិត (Survival); 2. សិទ្ធិទទួលបានការអភិវឌ្ឍ (Development); 3. សិទ្ធិទទួលបានការការពារ (Protection); 4. សិទ្ធិក្នុងការចូលរួម (Participation).
