# ScienceKH — Grade 7 (ថ្នាក់ទី ៧) National Curriculum Overview
## កម្មវិធីសិក្សាថ្នាក់ជាតិ ថ្នាក់ទី ៧ ក្រសួងអប់រំ យុវជន និងកីឡា

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Grade 7 (ថ្នាក់ទី ៧)** marks the transition from Primary Education (*បឋមសិក្សា*) into **Lower Secondary Education (*អនុវិទ្យាល័យ*)**. The curriculum consolidates foundational literacies, introduces formal scientific disciplines, expands mathematical abstraction, and develops civic responsibility.

## 2. Core Grade 7 Competencies Developed
By the end of Grade 7, Cambodian students must demonstrate:
1. **Mathematical Reasoning:** Perform operations on positive and negative integers, solve single-variable linear equations $ax+b=c$, calculate areas and perimeters of polygons, and interpret frequency tables.
2. **Scientific Inquiry:** Distinguish between physical and chemical changes, calculate density ($\rho = m/V$), identify cell organelles under optical microscopes, and explain the rock and water cycles.
3. **Linguistic & Literary Analysis:** Analyze syllable counts and rhyme schemes in traditional Kak-Kati poetry, identify Khmer word classes (nouns, verbs, adjectives), and compose structured descriptive paragraphs.
4. **Historical & Geographic Literacy:** Explain the archaeological origins of Cambodian civilization and analyze how the Mekong-Tonle Sap hydrological system supports human settlements.
5. **Civic & Digital Responsibility:** Understand the 4 core rights of the child, maintain respectful family relationships, and type fluently using the Khmer Unicode standard keyboard layout.
