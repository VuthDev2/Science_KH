## 1. Physics (រូបវិទ្យា ថ្នាក់ទី ៧)

### Subject Overview & Core Competencies:
Grade 7 Physics introduces foundational physical dimensions, metric SI units, matter density, mechanical advantage via simple machines (levers, pulleys, inclined planes), and thermal expansion.

### Units & Lessons Breakdown:
#### Unit 1: ការវាស់វែង ខ្នាត SI និងដង់ស៊ីតេ (Measurement, SI Units & Density)
- **Lesson 1 (`les-g7-phys-01`):** ការវាស់វែង និងប្រព័ន្ធខ្នាតអន្តរជាតិ SI (Measurement & SI Units)
  - *Main Concepts:* Length (meter - m), Mass (kilogram - kg), Time (second - s). Measurement uncertainty and parallax error avoidance.
- **Lesson 2 (`les-g7-phys-02`):** ម៉ាស មាឌ និងដង់ស៊ីតេនៃរូបធាតុ (Mass, Volume & Density)
  - *Formula:* $\rho = \frac{m}{V}$ (where $\rho$ is density in $\text{kg/m}^3$ or $\text{g/cm}^3$, $m$ is mass, and $V$ is volume).
  - *Worked Example:* An aluminum block has a mass of $54\text{ g}$ and a volume of $20\text{ cm}^3$. $\rho = \frac{54}{20} = 2.7\text{ g/cm}^3 = 2700\text{ kg/m}^3$.

#### Unit 2: ម៉ាស៊ីនសាមញ្ញ និងកម្ដៅ (Simple Machines & Thermal Expansion)
- **Lesson 3 (`les-g7-phys-03`):** ម៉ាស៊ីនសាមញ្ញ ឃ្នាស់ និងរ៉ក (Simple Machines: Levers & Pulleys)
  - *Main Concepts:* Law of the lever: $F_1 \times d_1 = F_2 \times d_2$. Fixed pulleys (change direction) vs movable pulleys (halve effort force).
- **Lesson 4 (`les-g7-phys-04`):** របងជម្រាល និងការងារមេកានិច (Inclined Planes & Work)
  - *Main Concepts:* Mechanical work $W = F \cdot d$. Using an inclined plane reduces the required lifting force by increasing travel distance.
- **Lesson 5 (`les-g7-phys-05`):** ការរីកមាឌនៃរូបធាតុដោយកម្តៅ (Thermal Expansion of Matter)
  - *Main Concepts:* Solids, liquids, and gases expand when heated and contract when cooled (gases expand most, solids least). Gaps in railway tracks prevent buckling.

## 2. Chemistry (គីមីវិទ្យា ថ្នាក់ទី ៧)

### Subject Overview & Core Competencies:
Investigates matter at the macroscopic and particulate scale, classifying pure substances versus homogeneous and heterogeneous mixtures, separation techniques, physical versus chemical transformations, and atmospheric air.

### Units & Lessons Breakdown:
#### Unit 1: សារធាតុបរិសុទ្ធ ល្បាយ និងវិធីញែក (Pure Substances, Mixtures & Separation)
- **Lesson 1 (`les-g7-chem-01`):** សារធាតុបរិសុទ្ធ និងល្បាយ (Pure Substances & Mixtures)
  - *Main Concepts:* Homogeneous mixtures (សូលុយស្យុងស្មើសាច់ — e.g. salt dissolved in water) vs Heterogeneous mixtures (ល្បាយមិនស្មើសាច់ — e.g. sand in water).
- **Lesson 2 (`les-g7-chem-02`):** វិធីសាស្រ្តញែកល្បាយ (Separation Techniques)
  - *Techniques:* ច្រោះ (Filtration for insolubles), រំហួត (Evaporation for soluble salts), បំណិត (Distillation based on boiling points).

#### Unit 2: បាតុភូតរូប គីមី ទឹក និងខ្យល់ (Physical/Chemical Changes, Water & Air)
- **Lesson 3 (`les-g7-chem-03`):** បាតុភូតរូប និងបាតុភូតគីមី (Physical vs Chemical Transformations)
  - *Main Concepts:* Physical changes alter state without creating new molecules (melting ice). Chemical changes break/form bonds and create new substances (rusting of iron, combustion).
- **Lesson 4 (`les-g7-chem-04`):** ទឹក សមាសភាព និងសារៈសំខាន់ (Water Properties & Hydrologic Cycle)
  - *Main Concepts:* Pure water formula $H_2O$, boiling point $100^\circ\text{C}$, freezing point $0^\circ\text{C}$ at 1 atm.
- **Lesson 5 (`les-g7-chem-05`):** ខ្យល់ និងឧស្ម័នក្នុងបរិយាកាស (Atmospheric Air & Oxygen)
  - *Composition:* Nitrogen ($N_2 \approx 78\%$), Oxygen ($O_2 \approx 21\%$), Argon and other gases ($\approx 1\%$), Carbon Dioxide ($CO_2 \approx 0.04\%$). Oxygen supports combustion and animal respiration.

## 3. Biology (ជីវវិទ្យា ថ្នាក់ទី ៧)

### Subject Overview & Core Competencies:
Introduces the cell as the basic unit of life, compares plant and animal cell organelles, explores the 5 kingdoms of living organisms, plant anatomy and photosynthesis, and food chains.

### Units & Lessons Breakdown:
#### Unit 1: កោសិកា និងចំណាត់ថ្នាក់ភាវៈរស់ (Cells & Classification of Living Organisms)
- **Lesson 1 (`les-g7-bio-01`):** រចនាសម្ព័ន្ធកោសិការុក្ខជាតិ និងសត្វ (Plant & Animal Cell Architecture)
  - *Organelles:* Nucleus (ខួរកោសិកា — genetic DNA), Cytoplasm (ស៊ីតូប្លាស), Cell membrane (ភ្នាសកោសិកា). Plant cells uniquely possess a rigid cellulose cell wall (កោសិការបាំង) and Chloroplasts (ក្លរ៉ូប្លាស) for photosynthesis.
- **Lesson 2 (`les-g7-bio-02`):** អាណាចក្រនៃភាវៈរស់ទាំង ៥ (The Five Kingdoms of Life)
  - *Taxa:* Monera (បាក់តេរី), Protista (ប្រូតូសូអែ), Fungi (ផ្សិត), Plantae (រុក្ខជាតិ), Animalia (សត្វ).

#### Unit 2: រុក្ខជាតិ និងច្រវាក់អាហារអេកូឡូស៊ី (Plant Physiology & Food Chains)
- **Lesson 3 (`les-g7-bio-03`):** សរីរាង្គរុក្ខជាតិ ឬស ដើម និងស្លឹក (Plant Anatomy: Roots, Stems, Leaves)
  - *Functions:* Roots absorb water and dissolved minerals; stems conduct fluids; leaves carry out gas exchange through stomata.
- **Lesson 4 (`les-g7-bio-04`):** ការសំយោគពន្លឺ និងការដកដង្ហើមរបស់រុក្ខជាតិ (Photosynthesis & Respiration)
  - *Equation:* $6CO_2 + 6H_2O + \text{ពន្លឺព្រះអាទិត្យ} \xrightarrow{\text{ក្លរ៉ូភីល}} C_6H_{12}O_6 (\text{គ្លុយកូស}) + 6O_2$.
- **Lesson 5 (`les-g7-bio-05`):** ច្រវាក់អាហារ និងបណ្តាញអាហារ (Food Chains & Ecological Webs)
  - *Trophic Levels:* ផលិតករ (Producers - green plants) $\to$ អតិថិជនថ្នាក់ទី១ (Primary consumers - herbivores) $\to$ អតិថិជនថ្នាក់ទី២ (Secondary consumers - carnivores) $\to$ អ្នកបំបែកធាតុ (Decomposers - fungi & bacteria).

## 4. Earth & Environmental Science (ផែនដី និងបរិស្ថានវិទ្យា ថ្នាក់ទី ៧)

### Subject Overview & Core Competencies:
Details the concentric structural layers of Planet Earth, the rock formation cycle, weathering and soil composition, the layered atmosphere, and local weather patterns in Cambodia.

### Units & Lessons Breakdown:
#### Unit 1: រចនាសម្ព័ន្ធផែនដី និងថ្ម (Earth's Internal Structure & The Rock Cycle)
- **Lesson 1 (`les-g7-earth-01`):** ស្រទាប់ខាងក្នុងនៃផែនដី (Concentric Layers of Earth)
  - *Layers:* សំបកផែនដី (Crust: 5–70 km), ម៉ង់តូ (Mantle: molten magma), ស្នូលក្រៅ (Liquid Outer Core), ស្នូលក្នុង (Solid Iron-Nickel Inner Core).
- **Lesson 2 (`les-g7-earth-02`):** វដ្តនៃថ្ម និងប្រភេទថ្ម (Rock Types & The Rock Cycle)
  - *Classes:* ថ្មម៉ាក់ម៉ា (Igneous - basalt, granite), ថ្មកម្ទេចកណកំណក (Sedimentary - sandstone, limestone), ថ្មប្រែរូប (Metamorphic - marble, slate).
- **Lesson 3 (`les-g7-earth-03`):** ដី សមាសភាព និងទម្រង់ដី (Soil Profiles & Soil Conservation)
  - *Composition:* Mineral particles (45%), Organic humus (5%), Water (25%), Air (25%).

#### Unit 2: បរិយាកាស និងការប្រែប្រួលអាកាសធាតុ (Atmosphere & Weather Systems)
- **Lesson 4 (`les-g7-earth-04`):** ស្រទាប់បរិយាកាសនៃផែនដី (Atmospheric Strata)
  - *Layers:* ត្រូប៉ូស្វ៊ែរ (Troposphere: weather phenomena), ស្ត្រាតូស្វ៊ែរ (Stratosphere: ozone layer $O_3$ shielding UV rays), មេសូស្វ៊ែរ (Mesosphere), ភែរម៉ូស្វ៊ែរ (Thermosphere).
- **Lesson 5 (`les-g7-earth-05`):** សម្ពាធបរិយាកាស ពពក និងទឹកភ្លៀង (Atmospheric Pressure, Clouds & Rainfall)
  - *Mechanics:* Barometers measure atmospheric pressure; warm moist air rises, condenses into clouds, and precipitates as monsoon rain.
