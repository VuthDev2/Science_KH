## 1. Physics (រូបវិទ្យា ថ្នាក់ទី ៨)

### Units & Lessons:
#### Unit 1: កម្លាំង និងសម្ពាធ (Forces & Pressure)
- **Lesson 1 (`les-g8-phys-01`):** កម្លាំង វ៉ិចទ័រកម្លាំង និងកម្លាំងកកិត (Forces & Friction)
  - *Main Concepts:* Force is an interaction measured in Newtons ($\text{N}$) with 4 characteristics: point of application, line of action, direction, and magnitude. Static vs kinetic friction.
- **Lesson 2 (`les-g8-phys-02`):** សម្ពាធក្នុងអង្គធាតុរឹង និងអង្គធាតុរាវ (Solid & Hydrostatic Pressure)
  - *Formulas:* Solid pressure $P = \frac{F}{A}$ (in Pascals, $\text{Pa} = \text{N/m}^2$); Hydrostatic pressure $P = \rho g h$ (increases with depth). Pascal's hydraulic press principle: $\frac{F_1}{A_1} = \frac{F_2}{A_2}$.

#### Unit 2: ការងារមេកានិច និងថាមពល (Mechanical Work & Energy)
- **Lesson 3 (`les-g8-phys-03`):** ការងារមេកានិច និងអានុភាព (Mechanical Work & Power)
  - *Formulas:* Work $W = F \cdot d$ (Joules, $\text{J}$); Power $P = \frac{W}{t}$ (Watts, $\text{W} = \text{J/s}$).
- **Lesson 4 (`les-g8-phys-04`):** កម្ដៅ និងការចម្លងកម្ដៅ (Heat Transfer Mechanics)
  - *Modes:* Conduction (ការចម្លងក្នុងអង្គធាតុរឹង), Convection (ចរន្តវិលវល់ក្នុងវត្ថុរាវនិងឧស្ម័ន), Radiation (រស្មីកម្តៅព្រះអាទិត្យ).
- **Lesson 5 (`les-g8-phys-05`):** សំឡេង និងរលកសំឡេង (Acoustics & Sound Waves)
  - *Main Concepts:* Sound propagates through mechanical vibrations in matter (speed in air $\approx 340\text{ m/s}$, faster in water $\approx 1500\text{ m/s}$ and steel $\approx 5000\text{ m/s}$). Cannot travel through a vacuum.

## 2. Chemistry (គីមីវិទ្យា ថ្នាក់ទី ៨)

### Units & Lessons:
#### Unit 1: ធាតុគីមី និងរូបមន្តគីមី (Elements, Symbols & Formulas)
- **Lesson 1 (`les-g8-chem-01`):** ធាតុគីមី និងនិមិត្តសញ្ញាគីមី (Chemical Elements & Symbols)
  - *Main Concepts:* Hydrogen ($H$), Carbon ($C$), Oxygen ($O$), Nitrogen ($N$), Sodium ($Na$), Chlorine ($Cl$), Iron ($Fe$), Copper ($Cu$).
- **Lesson 2 (`les-g8-chem-02`):** វ៉ាឡង់ និងរូបមន្តគីមី (Valence & Chemical Formulas)
  - *Valence Rules:* $H$ has valence I, $O$ has valence II. Cross-rule: $Al^{\text{III}}$ and $O^{\text{II}} \implies Al_2O_3$ (Aluminum oxide).

#### Unit 2: ច្បាប់រក្សាម៉ាស និងសមីការគីមី (Conservation of Mass & Chemical Equations)
- **Lesson 3 (`les-g8-chem-03`):** ច្បាប់រក្សាម៉ាស ឡាវ៉ូសៀ (Lavoisier's Law of Conservation of Mass)
  - *Statement:* "ក្នុងប្រតិកម្មគីមី គ្មានអ្វីកើតឡើងថ្មី គ្មានអ្វីបាត់បង់ឡើយ គ្រប់យ៉ាងគឺបំប្លែងទម្រង់" ($\Sigma m_{\text{reactants}} = \Sigma m_{\text{products}}$).
- **Lesson 4 (`les-g8-chem-04`):** សមីការគីមី និងការថ្លឹងសមីការ (Balancing Chemical Equations)
  - *Method:* Equalize number of each atom on both sides. $2H_2 + O_2 \to 2H_2O$; $CH_4 + 2O_2 \to CO_2 + 2H_2O$.
- **Lesson 5 (`les-g8-chem-05`):** អុកស៊ីតកម្ម និងចំហេះ (Oxidation & Combustion)
  - *Main Concepts:* Combustion requires fuel, oxidizer (oxygen), and ignition temperature. Complete combustion produces $CO_2$ and $H_2O$.

## 3. Biology (ជីវវិទ្យា ថ្នាក់ទី ៨)

### Units & Lessons:
#### Unit 1: ប្រព័ន្ធសរីរាង្គមនុស្ស (Human Body Systems)
- **Lesson 1 (`les-g8-bio-01`):** ប្រព័ន្ធរំលាយអាហារ (Human Digestive System)
  - *Organs:* Mouth (amylase enzyme) $\to$ Esophagus $\to$ Stomach (pepsin, gastric acid $\text{HCl}$) $\to$ Small intestine (nutrient absorption via villi) $\to$ Large intestine (water reabsorption).
- **Lesson 2 (`les-g8-bio-02`):** ប្រព័ន្ធដង្ហើម និងការផ្លាស់ប្តូរឧស្ម័ន (Respiratory System & Gas Exchange)
  - *Alveolar Exchange:* Oxygen diffuses from alveoli into capillary blood; carbon dioxide diffuses from blood into alveoli to be exhaled.
- **Lesson 3 (`les-g8-bio-03`):** ប្រព័ន្ធរបត់ឈាម និងបេះដូង (Cardiovascular System & The Heart)
  - *Chambers:* Left/right atria and ventricles. Arteries carry blood away from the heart; veins carry blood toward the heart.

#### Unit 2: ប្រព័ន្ធបញ្ចេញចោល និងការបន្តពូជ (Excretion & Reproduction)
- **Lesson 4 (`les-g8-bio-04`):** ប្រព័ន្ធបញ្ចេញចោល និងតម្រងនោម (Excretory System & Kidneys)
  - *Filtration:* Nephrons filter urea and metabolic wastes from blood to produce urine, maintaining water/salt homeostasis.
- **Lesson 5 (`les-g8-bio-05`):** ការបន្តពូជរបស់សត្វ (Animal Reproductive Strategies)
  - *Types:* Sexual reproduction (fusion of male sperm and female egg) vs Asexual reproduction (budding in hydra, regeneration in planaria).

## 4. Earth & Environmental Science (ផែនដីវិទ្យា ថ្នាក់ទី ៨)

### Units & Lessons:
#### Unit 1: ធនធានទឹកក្រោមដី និងរ៉ែ (Groundwater & Mineral Resources)
- **Lesson 1 (`les-g8-earth-01`):** ធនធានទឹកក្រោមដី និងអណ្តូងទឹក (Groundwater & Aquifers)
  - *Hydrology:* Water table, permeable gravel/sand layers, artesian wells, and over-extraction subsidence risks.
- **Lesson 2 (`les-g8-earth-02`):** ធនធានរ៉ែនៅកម្ពុជា (Cambodian Mineral Reserves)
  - *Distribution:* Iron ore (Kampong Thom), Gemstones (Pailin, Ratanakiri), Limestone (Kampot cement industry).
- **Lesson 3 (`les-g8-earth-03`):** ការសឹករិចរិលនៃដី និងការការពារ (Soil Erosion & Conservation)
  - *Mitigation:* Contour plowing, terracing on hillsides, cover cropping, and windbreak reforestation.

#### Unit 2: បាតុភូតភូគព្ភសាស្ត្រ (Geological Dynamics & Hazards)
- **Lesson 4 (`les-g8-earth-04`):** បន្ទាត់បាក់ស្រុត និងការរញ្ជួយដី (Fault Lines & Earthquakes)
  - *Mechanics:* Seismic wave generation along fault ruptures; measurement using the Richter magnitude scale and seismographs.
- **Lesson 5 (`les-g8-earth-05`):** ភ្នំភ្លើង និងបន្ទុះម៉ាក់ម៉ា (Volcanism & Magma Plumes)
  - *Landforms:* Active, dormant, and extinct volcanoes. In Cambodia, basaltic volcanic remnants form the fertile red soils of Ratanakiri and Kampong Cham.
