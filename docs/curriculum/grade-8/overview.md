# ScienceKH — Grade 8 (ថ្នាក់ទី ៨) National Curriculum Overview
## កម្មវិធីសិក្សាថ្នាក់ជាតិ ថ្នាក់ទី ៨ ក្រសួងអប់រំ យុវជន និងកីឡា

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Grade 8 (ថ្នាក់ទី ៨)** represents the intermediate development stage of Lower Secondary Education (*អនុវិទ្យាល័យ*). It consolidates Grade 7 foundational skills and directly constructs the algebraic, scientific, and literary scaffolding necessary for the Grade 9 National Diploma / Brevet Examination (*ការប្រឡងសញ្ញាបត្រមធ្យមសិក្សាបឋមភូមិ - ឌីប្លូម*).

---

## 1. Grade 8 Weekly Study Hours (MoEYS Standard Framework)

| No. | Subject (English) | Subject (Khmer) | Weekly Hours | Primary Curricular Focus |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Mathematics** | គណិតវិទ្យា | 6 | Polynomials, algebraic factoring, linear equations, triangle congruence, Pythagoras. |
| 2 | **Khmer Language & Literature** | ភាសាខ្មែរ | 6 | *Tum Teav* novel analysis, compound sentences, clause conjunctions, argumentative paragraphs. |
| 3 | **Physics** | រូបវិទ្យា | 2 | Forces, friction, solid/fluid pressure, mechanical work $W=Fd$, power. |
| 4 | **Chemistry** | គីមីវិទ្យា | 2 | Chemical symbols, valence, Lavoisier's conservation of mass, molecular formulas. |
| 5 | **Biology** | ជីវវិទ្យា | 2 | Human organ systems (digestive, respiratory, circulatory, excretory), animal reproduction. |
| 6 | **Earth & Environmental Science** | ផែនដី និងបរិស្ថានវិទ្យា | 2 | Groundwater aquifers, mineral reserves, soil erosion, geological faults, earthquakes. |
| 7 | **History** | ប្រវត្តិវិទ្យា | 2 | Chenla Kingdom (Land/Water), Jayavarman II 802 AD unification on Mt. Kulen, Angkor Wat. |
| 8 | **Geography** | ភូមិវិទ្យា | 2 | Natural resources, inland fisheries, wet vs dry season paddy rice, national irrigation. |
| 9 | **Moral & Civics** | សីលធម៌ និងពលរដ្ឋវិជ្ជា | 1.5 | Social solidarity, helping vulnerable groups, environmental responsibility, etiquette. |
| 10 | **English** | ភាសាអង់គ្លេស | 3.5 | Modal verbs (*must, should, can*), comparatives, past continuous, narrative writing. |
| 11 | **ICT & Computing** | ព័ត៌មានវិទ្យា | 1 | Electronic spreadsheets (Excel/Sheets), cell addressing, formulas (SUM, AVERAGE), charts. |
| 12 | **Physical Education & Health** | អប់រំកាយ និងសុខភាព | 2 | Track & field athletics, volleyball fundamentals, adolescent health, nutrition. |
| 13 | **Arts & Local Life Skills** | សិល្បៈ និងបំណិនជីវិត | 1 | Traditional music instruments, applied handicraft, local agricultural techniques. |
| **Total** | | | **33 Hours/Week** | **Intermediate Lower Secondary Framework** |
