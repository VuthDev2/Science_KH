# ScienceKH — Grade 12 (ថ្នាក់ទី ១២) National Bac II Curriculum Overview
## កម្មវិធីសិក្សាថ្នាក់ជាតិ ថ្នាក់ទី ១២ និងការប្រឡងសញ្ញាបត្រមធ្យមសិក្សាទុតិយភូមិ (បាក់ឌុប)

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Grade 12 (ថ្នាក់ទី ១២)** is the apex year of General Secondary Education in the Kingdom of Cambodia. It culminates in the **National Baccalaureate II Examination (*ការប្រឡងសញ្ញាបត្រមធ្យមសិក្សាទុតិយភូមិ - បាក់ឌុប*)** administered nationwide by the Ministry of Education, Youth and Sport (MoEYS).

---

## 1. Official National Baccalaureate II (បាក់ឌុប) 500-Point Examination Matrix

MoEYS sets an exact **500-point ceiling** with distinctive subject coefficients tailored to each stream:

|---|---|---|:---:|:---:|---|
| 1 | **Mathematics** | គណិតវិទ្យា | **125 Marks** | 150 Mins | Integrals, volumes of revolution, differential equations, complex numbers, 3D space geometry, random variables. |
| 2 | **Physics** | រូបវិទ្យា | **75 Marks** | 90 Mins | Free oscillations (pendulums), waves, AC resonance (RLC), nuclear radioactivity & $E=mc^2$. |

## 2. Official MoEYS National Grading Scale (និទ្ទេសបាក់ឌុប)
- **Grade A (និទ្ទេស A):** $\ge 450 \text{ to } 500 \text{ Marks}$ ($\ge 90\%$) — Superior Excellence (*ល្អប្រសើរ*)
- **Grade B (និទ្ទេស B):** $\ge 400 \text{ to } 449 \text{ Marks}$ ($\ge 80\%$) — Very Good (*ល្អណាស់*)
- **Grade C (និទ្ទេស C):** $\ge 350 \text{ to } 399 \text{ Marks}$ ($\ge 70\%$) — Good (*ល្អ*)
- **Grade D (និទ្ទេស D):** $\ge 300 \text{ to } 349 \text{ Marks}$ ($\ge 60\%$) — Fairly Good (*ល្អបង្គួរ*)
- **Grade E (និទ្ទេស E):** $\ge 250 \text{ to } 299 \text{ Marks}$ ($\ge 50\%$) — Passing Grade (*មធ្យម*)
- **Grade F (និទ្ទេស F):** $< 250 \text{ Marks}$ ($< 50\%$) — Unsatisfactory / Failed (*ធ្លាក់*)
