# ScienceKH — Grade 9 (ថ្នាក់ទី ៩) National Brevet Curriculum Overview
## កម្មវិធីសិក្សាថ្នាក់ជាតិ ថ្នាក់ទី ៩ និងការប្រឡងសញ្ញាបត្រមធ្យមសិក្សាបឋមភូមិ (ឌីប្លូម)

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Grade 9 (ថ្នាក់ទី ៩)** is the culminating terminal year of **Lower Secondary Education (*អនុវិទ្យាល័យ*)** in Cambodia. It prepares students for the provincial **National Lower Secondary Diploma Examination (*ការប្រឡងសញ្ញាបត្រមធ្យមសិក្សាបឋមភូមិ - ឌីប្លូម / Brevet*)** administered under the authority of MoEYS.

---

## 1. Grade 9 National Diploma Examination (ឌីប្លូម) Subject Matrix

The Brevet examination evaluates cumulative mastery across the three-year junior secondary cycle:

| Examination Subject | Subject (Khmer) | Format | Marks | Key Exam Topics Tested |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Mathematics** | គណិតវិទ្យា | Written Problem-Solving | 100 | 2-variable linear systems, quadratic equations, Viète relations, Thales theorem, right-triangle trig, Laplace probability. |
| **Khmer Literature** | ភាសាខ្មែរ | Textual Analysis & Essay | 100 | *Kulap Pailin* character virtues, grammatical transformation, 3-tier expository/descriptive essay. |
| **Physics** | រូបវិទ្យា | Written Assessment | 50 | Ohm's law ($U=RI$), electric energy/power ($P=UI$), circuits, reflection/refraction laws. |
| **Chemistry** | គីមីវិទ្យា | Written Assessment | 50 | Mole calculations ($n=m/M$), molarity ($C=n/V$), pH scale, neutralization equations, metal reactivity. |
| **Biology** | ជីវវិទ្យា | Written Assessment | 50 | Human nervous system (neurons, reflex arcs), endocrine hormones, sensory organs, chromosomes. |
| **Earth Science** | ផែនដីវិទ្យា | Written Assessment | 50 | Ozone layer depletion, greenhouse effect, Cambodian mineral deposits, renewable solar/hydro energy. |
| **History** | ប្រវត្តិវិទ្យា | Written Essay / Questions | 50 | Peak of Angkor (Jayavarman VII), French Protectorate 1863, Royal Independence Crusade (Nov 9, 1953). |
| **Geography** | ភូមិវិទ្យា | Written Essay / Questions | 50 | Cambodian demography, population pyramids, garment manufacturing, export trade corridors. |
| **Moral & Civics** | សីលធម៌-ពលរដ្ឋ | Written Questions | 50 | Traffic safety laws, National Assembly, Senate, Royal Government, voting rights at age 18. |
| **English** | ភាសាអង់គ្លេស | Reading & Grammar | 50 | Passive voice, modal auxiliaries, paragraph composition, reading comprehension. |
| **Total Marks** | | | **600 Points** | **Pass Threshold: ≥ 300 Points** |

## 2. Weekly Study Allocation (Table 4 MoEYS Framework)
- Mathematics: 6 Hours / Week
- Khmer Literature: 6 Hours / Week
- Sciences (Physics, Chemistry, Biology, Earth Science): 8 Hours / Week (2 hrs each)
- Social Studies (History, Geography, Moral & Civics): 5.5 Hours / Week
- English: 3.5 Hours / Week
- ICT & Life Skills: 2 Hours / Week
- Physical Education: 2 Hours / Week
- **Total: 33 Hours / Week**
