## 1. Khmer Language & Literature (ភាសាខ្មែរ ថ្នាក់ទី ១០)

### Key Units & Lessons:
#### Unit 1: រឿងរាមកេរ្តិ៍ និងកាព្យបុរាណព្រហ្មគីតិ (Reamker Epic & Brahmagit Prosody)
- **Lesson 1 (`les-g10-khmer-01`):** រឿងរាមកេរ្តិ៍ (កម្រិតវិភាគទស្សនវិជ្ជា) (Philosophical Analysis of the Reamker)
  - *Context:* Khmer adaptation of the ancient epic, carved on Angkor Wat bas-reliefs.
  - *Dharmic Philosophy:* Eternal moral struggle between Dharma (ព្រះធម៌ — righteousness embodied by Preah Ream) and Adharma (អធម៌ — tyrannical greed embodied by Krong Reap). Demonstrates the ideal moral conduct of royal leadership and martial fidelity.
- **Lesson 2 (`les-g10-khmer-02`):** កាព្យបទព្រហ្មគីតិ និងឥន្ទ្រជិត (Brahmagit & Indrajit Poetic Meters)
  - *Brahmagit Structure:* 4 phrases per stanza. Line 1: 5 syllables; Line 2: 6 syllables; Line 3: 5 syllables; Line 4: 6 syllables (22 syllables total). Syllable 5 of line 1 rhymes with syllable 3 of line 2. Used traditionally in didactic moral codes (*សាស្ត្រាច្បាប់*).

## 2. History (ប្រវត្តិវិទ្យា ថ្នាក់ទី ១០)

### Key Units & Lessons:
#### Unit 1: សម័យកណ្តាល ពាណិជ្ជកម្មលង្វែក និងសម័យឧដុង្គ (Longvek & Oudong Eras)
- **Lesson 1 (`les-g10-hist-01`):** សម័យកណ្តាល (ចតុមុខ លង្វែក និងឧដុង្គ) (Post-Angkorian Capitals)
  - *Strategic Shift:* Relocation of the royal capital from Angkor southward to Chaktomuk (Phnom Penh) under King Ponhea Yat, securing confluence river access.
- **Lesson 2 (`les-g10-hist-02`):** សេដ្ឋកិច្ចពាណិជ្ជកម្មលង្វែក និងសម័យឧដុង្គ (Longvek Entrepôt & Oudong)
  - *International Commerce:* Fortress-city of Longvek under King Ang Chan I developed into a thriving global commercial port, trading spices, fragrant woods, and elephants with Portuguese, Spanish, Dutch, and Japanese merchants.

## 3. Geography (ភូមិវិទ្យា ថ្នាក់ទី ១០)

### Key Units & Lessons:
#### Unit 1: ធនធានធម្មជាតិ និងការអភិរក្សជីវចម្រុះ (Resources & Protected Areas)
- **Lesson 1 (`les-g10-geo-01`):** ធនធានរ៉ែ ថាមពល និងកសិកម្មកម្ពុជា (Mining, Energy & Agriculture)
  - *Strategic Assets:* Bauxite deposits in Mondulkiri; iron ore reserves in Kampong Thom; clean hydropower reservoirs (Kamchay, Russei Chrum, Stung Tatay) supplying national grid electricity.
- **Lesson 2 (`les-g10-geo-02`):** ការអភិរក្សជីវចម្រុះ និងតំបន់ការពារធម្មជាតិ (Protected Areas & Biodiversity)
  - *Network:* Cambodia's protected area system covers over 40% of the terrestrial territory: Kirirom National Park, Bokor National Park, Phnom Kulen National Park, Southern Cardamom Wildlife Sanctuary, and Ramsar wetland sites (Koh Kapik, Stung Treng).

## 4. English as a Foreign Language (ភាសាអង់គ្លេស ថ្នាក់ទី ១០)

### Key Units & Lessons:
#### Unit 1: Academic Grammar, Perfect Tenses & Conditionals
- **Lesson 1 (`les-g10-eng-01`):** Present Perfect vs Past Simple in Academic Texts
  - *Contrasts:* Present Perfect (*has/have + V3*) connects past discoveries to present relevance; Past Simple (*-ed*) identifies specific historical experimental dates.
- **Lesson 2 (`les-g10-eng-02`):** Conditionals (Type 1 & 2) & Academic Connectors
  - *First Conditional:* Real present/future possibility (*If + present simple, will + base verb*).
  - *Second Conditional:* Hypothetical present (*If + past simple, would + base verb* - using *were* in formal prose).
  - *Discourse Connectors:* *Consequently, furthermore, in contrast, therefore*.
