# ScienceKH — Grade 10 (ថ្នាក់ទី ១០) National Curriculum Overview
## កម្មវិធីសិក្សាថ្នាក់ជាតិ ថ្នាក់ទី ១០ ក្រសួងអប់រំ យុវជន និងកីឡា

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Grade 10 (ថ្នាក់ទី ១០)** is the foundational gateway year of **Upper Secondary Education (*វិទ្យាល័យ*)**. Under the MoEYS Curriculum Framework, Grade 10 delivers a shared core curriculum across STEM and Social Sciences, serving as the diagnostic benchmark that guides students in choosing their Grade 11–12 stream: **ថ្នាក់វិទ្យាសាស្ត្រ (Science Stream)** or **ថ្នាក់វិទ្យាសាស្ត្រសង្គម (Social Science Stream)**.

## 1. Grade 10 Weekly Study Hours (MoEYS Core Curriculum)

| No. | Subject (English) | Subject (Khmer) | Weekly Hours | Primary Curricular Focus |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Mathematics** | គណិតវិទ្យា | 6 | Polynomial roots, quadratic trinomial signs, trigonometric unit circle, planar vectors. |
| 2 | **Khmer Language & Literature** | ភាសាខ្មែរ | 5 | *Reamker* classical epic analysis, Brahmagit poetic meter, expository essay structure. |
| 3 | **Physics** | រូបវិទ្យា | 3 | Kinematics ($v = v_0 + at$), Newton's three laws, mechanical work and energy conservation. |
| 4 | **Chemistry** | គីមីវិទ្យា | 3 | Atomic orbitals ($1s^2 2s^2 2p^6$), periodic law, chemical bonding (covalent/ionic), stoichiometry. |
| 5 | **Biology** | ជីវវិទ្យា | 3 | Cellular biomolecules, enzyme kinetics, cell division (mitosis and meiosis), respiration. |
| 6 | **Earth & Environmental Science** | ផែនដីវិទ្យា | 2 | Plate tectonics, continental drift, earthquakes, ocean currents, and climate systems. |
| 7 | **History** | ប្រវត្តិវិទ្យា | 2 | Post-Angkorian Middle Period, Longvek international maritime trade, Oudong dynasty. |

| 9 | **Moral & Civics** | សីលធម៌-ពលរដ្ឋ | 1.5 | Universal Declaration of Human Rights (UDHR), gender equality, peaceful dispute resolution. |

## 2. Stream Orientation Guidance (End of Grade 10 Decision)
At the conclusion of Grade 10, students select their specialized pathway based on academic performance and career aspirations:
- **Choose Science Stream (ថ្នាក់វិទ្យាសាស្ត្រ) if:** Seeking university degrees in Medicine, Pharmacy, Civil/Electrical Engineering, Computer Science, Architecture, Agriculture, or Pure Sciences.
- **Choose Social Science Stream (ថ្នាក់វិទ្យាសាស្ត្រសង្គម) if:** Seeking university degrees in Law, International Relations, Economics, Banking, Journalism, Education, Public Administration, Literature, or Tourism.
