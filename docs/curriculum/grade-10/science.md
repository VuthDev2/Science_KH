## 1. Physics (រូបវិទ្យា ថ្នាក់ទី ១០)

### Key Units & Lessons:
#### Unit 1: ស៊ីនេម៉ាទិចនៃចលនាត្រង់ (Kinematics of Linear Motion)
- **Lesson 1 (`les-g10-phys-01`):** ចលនាត្រង់ស្មើ និងចលនាប្រែប្រួលស្មើ (Linear Kinematics)
  - *Equations:*
    - Uniform motion: $x = x_0 + vt$.
    - Uniformly accelerated motion: $v = v_0 + at$; $x = x_0 + v_0t + \frac{1}{2}at^2$; $v^2 - v_0^2 = 2a(x - x_0)$.

#### Unit 2: ឌីណាមិច និងច្បាប់ញូតុន (Dynamics & Newton's Laws)
- **Lesson 2 (`les-g10-phys-02`):** ច្បាប់ទាំងបីរបស់ញូតុន (Newton's Three Laws of Motion)
  - *First Law (Inertia):* In an inertial frame, an object at rest remains at rest, and an object in motion continues with constant velocity unless acted upon by a net force ($\Sigma \vec{F} = \vec{0} \implies \vec{a} = \vec{0}$).
  - *Second Law (Fundamental Equation):* $\Sigma \vec{F} = m \vec{a}$.
  - *Third Law (Action-Reaction):* $\vec{F}_{A/B} = -\vec{F}_{B/A}$.
- **Lesson 3 (`les-g10-phys-03`):** ថាមពលមេកានិច និងច្បាប់រក្សាថាមពល (Mechanical Energy Conservation)
  - *Energy:* Kinetic energy $E_k = \frac{1}{2}mv^2$; Gravitational potential energy $E_p = mgh$. Mechanical energy $E_m = E_k + E_p = \text{constant}$ in conservative systems.

## 2. Chemistry (គីមីវិទ្យា ថ្នាក់ទី ១០)

### Key Units & Lessons:
#### Unit 1: អាតូម និងតារាងខួបនៃធាតុគីមី (Atomic Orbitals & Periodic Trends)
- **Lesson 1 (`les-g10-chem-01`):** រចនាសម្ព័ន្ធអេឡិចត្រុងក្នុងអាតូម (Electron Configurations)
  - *Subshells:* Energy levels $n = 1, 2, 3, 4$; Subshells $s$ (max 2 electrons), $p$ (max 6), $d$ (max 10), $f$ (max 14).
  - *Aufbau Principle:* Order: $1s \to 2s \to 2p \to 3s \to 3p \to 4s \to 3d...$
  - *Example:* Sodium ($Na, Z=11$): $1s^2 2s^2 2p^6 3s^1$.
- **Lesson 2 (`les-g10-chem-02`):** តារាងខួបនៃធាតុគីមី និងបម្រែបម្រួលលក្ខណៈ (Periodic Table Properties)
  - *Trends:* In a period (left to right): atomic radius decreases, electronegativity increases. In a group (top to bottom): atomic radius increases, metallic character increases.

#### Unit 2: សម្ព័ន្ធគីមី (Chemical Bonding)
- **Lesson 3 (`les-g10-chem-03`):** សម្ព័ន្ធកូវ៉ាឡង់ និងសម្ព័ន្ធអ៊ីយ៉ុង (Covalent vs Ionic Bonding)
  - *Covalent Bond:* Sharing of valence electron pairs between non-metals ($H_2, O_2, CH_4$).
  - *Ionic Bond:* Electrostatic attraction following electron transfer between metal cation and non-metal anion ($Na^+ + Cl^- \to NaCl$).

## 3. Biology (ជីវវិទ្យា ថ្នាក់ទី ១០)

### Key Units & Lessons:
#### Unit 1: ម៉ូលេគុលជីវៈ និងអង់ស៊ីម (Biomolecules & Enzymes)
- **Lesson 1 (`les-g10-bio-01`):** ម៉ូលេគុលសរីរាង្គក្នុងកោសិកា (Cellular Biomolecules)
  - *Four Classes:* Carbohydrates (monosaccharides, polysaccharides like glycogen/cellulose); Lipids (fatty acids, triglycerides, phospholipid bilayers); Proteins (amino acid peptide polymers); Nucleic Acids (DNA & RNA nucleotides).
- **Lesson 2 (`les-g10-bio-02`):** អង់ស៊ីម និងកាតាលីករជីវសាស្រ្ត (Enzyme Action & Kinetics)
  - *Lock-and-Key Model:* Active site binds specific substrate, lowering activation energy ($E_a$). Denatured by excessive heat or extreme pH.

#### Unit 2: ការបន្តពូជកោសិកា និងថាមពល (Cell Division & Bioenergetics)
- **Lesson 3 (`les-g10-bio-03`):** វដ្តកោសិកា មីតូស និងមេយ៉ូស (Mitosis vs Meiosis)
  - *Mitosis:* One $2n$ diploid cell produces two identical $2n$ somatic cells (prophase, metaphase, anaphase, telophase).
  - *Meiosis:* One $2n$ germ cell produces four non-identical $n$ haploid gametes (sperm/egg), enabling crossing-over genetic recombination.

## 4. Earth Science (ផែនដីវិទ្យា ថ្នាក់ទី ១០)

### Key Units & Lessons:
#### Unit 1: ផ្លាកតិចតូនិច និងភូគព្ភសាស្ត្រថាមវន្ត (Plate Tectonics & Marine Dynamics)
- **Lesson 1 (`les-g10-earth-01`):** ទ្រឹស្តីផ្លាកតិចតូនិច និងទ្វីបរសាត់ (Plate Tectonics & Continental Drift)
  - *Boundary Types:* Convergent boundaries (ការរំកិលបុកគ្នា — subduction trenches and mountain building), Divergent boundaries (ការរំកិលញែកចេញ — mid-ocean ridges), Transform faults (San Andreas fault).
