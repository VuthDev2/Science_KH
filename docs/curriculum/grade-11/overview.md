# ScienceKH — Grade 11 (ថ្នាក់ទី ១១) Stream Specialization Overview
## កម្មវិធីសិក្សាថ្នាក់ជាតិ ថ្នាក់ទី ១១៖ ការបែងចែកខ្សែទិស វិទ្យាសាស្ត្រ និងវិទ្យាសាស្ត្រសង្គម

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Grade 11 (ថ្នាក់ទី ១១)** initiates the formal two-year academic specialization stream in Cambodian Upper Secondary Education. Students follow dedicated curricula tailored to their track, preparing directly for the respective examination structures of the National Baccalaureate II (បាក់ឌុប).

---

## 1. Weekly Study Allocations by Academic Stream (MoEYS Framework)

### Stream Comparison Matrix (ម៉ោងសិក្សាប្រចាំសប្តាហ៍):

| Subject | Subject (Khmer) | ថ្នាក់វិទ្យាសាស្ត្រ (Science Stream) | ថ្នាក់វិទ្យាសាស្ត្រសង្គម (Social Science Stream) | Curricular Emphasis |
|---|---|:---:|:---:|---|
| **Mathematics** | គណិតវិទ្យា | **6 Hours** (Advanced Track) | **5 Hours** (General Track) | Limits, derivatives, sequences, permutations, probability. |
| **Khmer Literature** | ភាសាខ្មែរ | **5 Hours** | **6 Hours** (Major Track) | *Sim the Driver*, *Mealea Doung Chett*, discursive essay synthesis. |
| **Physics** | រូបវិទ្យា | **4 Hours** (Major Track) | **2 Hours** (Subsidiary) | Rotational mechanics, thermodynamics, fluid dynamics. |
| **Chemistry** | គីមីវិទ្យា | **4 Hours** (Major Track) | **2 Hours** (Subsidiary) | Enthalpy change, reaction kinetics, organic hydrocarbons, alcohols. |
| **Biology** | ជីវវិទ្យា | **4 Hours** (Major Track) | **2 Hours** (Subsidiary) | Circulatory system, blood types, plant xylem/phloem transport. |
| **Earth Science** | ផែនដីវិទ្យា | **2 Hours** (Subsidiary) | **2 Hours** | Historical geology, paleontology, meteorology, EIA assessments. |
| **History** | ប្រវត្តិវិទ្យា | **2 Hours** (Subsidiary) | **4 Hours** (Major Track) | Anticolonial uprisings (Achar Sva, Kralahom Kong), WWI & WWII in Asia. |
| **Geography** | ភូមិវិទ្យា | **2 Hours** (Subsidiary) | **4 Hours** (Major Track) | Urban growth, SEZs, expressway networks, deep-water port logistics. |
| **Moral & Civics** | សីលធម៌-ពលរដ្ឋ | **1 Hour** | **2 Hours** (Major Track) | Cultural heritage preservation, labor rights, professional ethics. |
| **Foreign Language (English)** | ភាសាអង់គ្លេស | **4 Hours** | **5 Hours** | Academic reading, relative clauses, conditionals, scientific texts. |
| **Physical Education** | អប់រំកាយ | **2 Hours** | **2 Hours** | Team sports, tactical gameplay, health fitness. |
| **Total** | | **36 Hours / Week** | **36 Hours / Week** | **Specialized Academic Rigor** |
