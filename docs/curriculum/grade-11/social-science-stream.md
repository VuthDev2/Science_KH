## 1. Khmer Language & Literature (ភាសាខ្មែរ ថ្នាក់ទី ១១ វិទ្យាសាស្ត្រសង្គម)

### Units & Lessons Breakdown:
#### Unit 1: អក្សរសិល្ប៍ទំនើប៖ ស៊ីមអ្នកបរឡាន និងមាលាដួងចិត្ត (Modern Realism Masterpieces)
- **Lesson 1 (`les-g11-khmer-01`):** រឿងស៊ីមអ្នកបរឡាន (អក្សរសិល្ប៍ទំនើប) (Sim the Driver Analysis)
  - *Context:* Written by Im Chhom in 1956, portraying the lived struggles of the urban working class in mid-20th-century Cambodia.
  - *Themes:* Class solidarity, labor exploitation, and progressive awakening among transport workers without resorting to destructive violence.
- **Lesson 2 (`les-g11-khmer-02`):** រឿងមាលាដួងចិត្ត (ស្នាដៃ នូ ហាច) (Mealea Doung Chett Novel Analysis)
  - *Context:* Nou Hach's epistolary novel exploring Cambodian students in France during the 1940s.
  - *Themes:* Intellectual patriotism, modern romance restrained by cultural ethics, and the duty of educated youth to return and modernize the motherland.

## 2. History (ប្រវត្តិវិទ្យា ថ្នាក់ទី ១១ វិទ្យាសាស្ត្រសង្គម)

### Units & Lessons Breakdown:
#### Unit 1: សម័យអាណាព្យាបាលបារាំង និងចលនាតស៊ូខ្មែរ (French Rule & Resistance)
- **Lesson 1 (`les-g11-hist-01`):** សម័យអាណាព្យាបាលបារាំងនៅកម្ពុជា (១៨៦៣-១៩៥៣) (French Colonial Era)
  - *Administration:* Treaty of August 11, 1863; administrative centralization; 1884 Convention subordinating royal governance to French Residents; fiscal monopolies on salt, alcohol, and opium.
- **Lesson 2 (`les-g11-hist-02`):** ចលនាតស៊ូប្រឆាំងបារាំង និងឥទ្ធិពលសង្គ្រាមលោក (Anticolonial Resistance)
  - *Movements:* 1865 Achar Sva peasant revolt; 1885 Kralahom Kong armed insurrection; July 20, 1942 Umbrella Demonstration (*បាតុកម្មឆ័ត្រ*) in Phnom Penh led by Venerable Hem Chiev and Pach Chhoeun demanding national autonomy.

## 3. Geography (ភូមិវិទ្យា ថ្នាក់ទី ១១ វិទ្យាសាស្ត្រសង្គម)

### Units & Lessons Breakdown:
#### Unit 1: នគរូបនីយកម្ម តំបន់សេដ្ឋកិច្ចពិសេស និងហេដ្ឋារចនាសម្ព័ន្ធ (Logistics & SEZs)
- **Lesson 1 (`les-g11-geo-01`):** នគរូបនីយកម្ម និងតំបន់សេដ្ឋកិច្ចពិសេស (SEZ) (Urban Growth & SEZs)
  - *Industrial Zones:* Phnom Penh Special Economic Zone (PPSEZ), Manhattan SEZ (Bavet), and Sihanoukville SEZ attracting foreign direct investment (FDI) in automotive wiring, electronics, and precision tooling.
- **Lesson 2 (`les-g11-geo-02`):** ហេដ្ឋារចនាសម្ព័ន្ធដឹកជញ្ជូន ផ្លូវល្បឿនលឿន និងកំពង់ផែទឹកជ្រៅ (Transport Corridors)
  - *Logistics Infrastructure:* Phnom Penh-Sihanoukville Expressway (E1); Sihanoukville Autonomous Port (PAS) deep-water container terminal expansion; international Class 4F aviation hubs.

## 4. Moral & Civics (សីលធម៌ និងពលរដ្ឋវិជ្ជា ថ្នាក់ទី ១១ វិទ្យាសាស្ត្រសង្គម)

### Core Units & Competencies:
- **National Cultural Heritage:** Legal and ethical protection of UNESCO world heritage monuments (Angkor, Preah Vihear, Sambor Prei Kuk, Koh Ker) and intangible cultural heritage (Royal Ballet, Sbek Thom, Chapei Dang Veng, Kun Lbokator).
- **Labor Standards & Ethics:** The Cambodian Labor Law, workplace non-discrimination, fair minimum wages, occupational safety, and professional codes of conduct.
