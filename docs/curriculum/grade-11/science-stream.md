## 1. Advanced Mathematics (គណិតវិទ្យាកម្រិតខ្ពស់ ថ្នាក់ទី ១១)

### Units & Lessons Breakdown:
#### Unit 1: លីមីតនៃអនុគមន៍ និងភាពជាប់ (Limits & Continuity)
- **Lesson 1 (`les-g11-math-01`):** លីមីតនៃអនុគមន៍ និងទម្រង់មិនកំណត់ (Limits of Functions)
  - *Indeterminate Forms:* $\left[\frac{0}{0}\right]$, $\left[\frac{\infty}{\infty}\right]$, $[\infty - \infty]$, $[0 \times \infty]$.
  - *Rational Limits:* Factor numerator and denominator to cancel vanishing factors $(x - x_0)$.
  - *Trigonometric Fundamental Limit:* $\lim_{x \to 0} \frac{\sin x}{x} = 1$; $\lim_{x \to 0} \frac{1 - \cos x}{x^2} = \frac{1}{2}$.
- **Lesson 2 (`les-g11-math-02`):** ភាពជាប់នៃអនុគមន៍ត្រង់ចំណុច និងលើចន្លោះ (Continuity of Functions)
  - *Definition:* Function $f(x)$ is continuous at $x_0$ if: 1. $f(x_0)$ exists; 2. $\lim_{x \to x_0} f(x)$ exists; 3. $\lim_{x \to x_0} f(x) = f(x_0)$.
  - *Intermediate Value Theorem (IVT):* If $f$ is continuous on $[a, b]$ and $f(a) \cdot f(b) < 0$, then there exists at least one $c \in (a, b)$ such that $f(c) = 0$.

#### Unit 2: ដេរីវេ ស្វ៊ីត និងប្រូបាប៊ីលីតេ (Derivatives, Sequences & Probability)
- **Lesson 3 (`les-g11-math-03`):** រូបមន្តដេរីវេ និងការអនុវត្តរកបរមា (Differentiation & Curve Analysis)
  - *Master Rules:* $(u \pm v)' = u' \pm v'$; $(uv)' = u'v + uv'$; $\left(\frac{u}{v}\right)' = \frac{u'v - uv'}{v^2}$; $(u^n)' = n u^{n-1} u'$.
  - *Sign of $f'(x)$:* If $f'(x) > 0 \implies f$ is strictly increasing; If $f'(x) < 0 \implies f$ is strictly decreasing; Extrema occur where $f'(x_0) = 0$ with a sign change.
- **Lesson 4 (`les-g11-math-04`):** ស្វ៊ីតនព្វន្ត និងស្វ៊ីតធរណីមាត្រ (Arithmetic & Geometric Progressions)
  - *Arithmetic:* Common difference $d = u_{n+1} - u_n$; $n$-th term $u_n = u_1 + (n - 1)d$; Sum $S_n = \frac{n(u_1 + u_n)}{2}$.
  - *Geometric:* Common ratio $q = \frac{u_{n+1}}{u_n}$; $n$-th term $u_n = u_1 \cdot q^{n-1}$; Sum $S_n = u_1 \frac{1 - q^n}{1 - q}$ ($q \ne 1$).
- **Lesson 5 (`les-g11-math-05`):** បន្សំ ចម្លាស់ និងប្រូបាប៊ីលីតេ (Combinatorics & Probability)
  - *Formulas:* Permutations $P_n = n!$; Arrangements $A_n^k = \frac{n!}{(n-k)!}$; Combinations $C_n^k = \frac{n!}{k!(n-k)!}$.

## 2. Advanced Physics (រូបវិទ្យាកម្រិតខ្ពស់ ថ្នាក់ទី ១១)

### Units & Lessons Breakdown:
#### Unit 1: ចលនារង្វិល និងកម្លាំងរមួល (Rotational Mechanics & Torque)
- **Core Formulas:** Angular velocity $\omega = \frac{d\theta}{dt}$; Angular acceleration $\alpha = \frac{d\omega}{dt}$; Torque $\tau = r F \sin\theta = I \alpha$ (where $I$ is moment of inertia).
- **Angular Momentum:** $L = I\omega = \text{constant}$ in the absence of external torque.

#### Unit 2: ទែម៉ូឌីណាមិច និងច្បាប់ឧស្ម័ន (Thermodynamics & Ideal Gas)
- **Equation of State:** $PV = nRT$ (where $R = 8.314\text{ J/(mol}\cdot\text{K)}$).
- **First Law of Thermodynamics:** $\Delta U = Q + W$ (change in internal energy equals heat supplied plus work done on the system).

## 3. Advanced Chemistry (គីមីវិទ្យាកម្រិតខ្ពស់ ថ្នាក់ទី ១១)

### Units & Lessons Breakdown:
#### Unit 1: ទែម៉ូគីមី និងល្បឿនប្រតិកម្ម (Thermochemistry & Rates)
- **Enthalpy Change ($\Delta H$):** Exothermic ($\Delta H < 0$, releases heat to surroundings) vs Endothermic ($\Delta H > 0$, absorbs heat).

#### Unit 2: គីមីសរីរាង្គ អ៊ីដ្រូកាបួ និងអាកុល (Organic Hydrocarbons & Alcohols)
- **Alkanes ($C_n H_{2n+2}$):** Saturated hydrocarbons undergoing free radical substitution.
- **Alkenes ($C_n H_{2n}$):** Unsaturated hydrocarbons containing a $C=C$ double bond undergoing addition reactions ($+Br_2, +H_2O$).
- **Alcohols ($R-OH$):** Functional group hydroxyl, oxidized to aldehydes and carboxylic acids.

## 4. Advanced Biology (ជីវវិទ្យាកម្រិតខ្ពស់ ថ្នាក់ទី ១១)

### Units & Lessons Breakdown:
#### Unit 1: ប្រព័ន្ធរបត់ឈាមមនុស្ស និងភាពស៊ាំ (Human Hemodynamics & Immunity)
- **Blood Components:** Red blood cells (erythrocytes with hemoglobin for $O_2$ transport), White blood cells (leukocytes for immune defense), Platelets (thrombocytes for clotting), Blood plasma.
- **ABO Blood Groups & Rh Factor:** Antigens on erythrocyte membrane; universal donor $O^-$, universal recipient $AB^+$.

#### Unit 2: សរីរវិទ្យារុក្ខជាតិ (Plant Vascular Physiology)
- **Xylem (សរសៃឈើ):** Transports water and inorganic minerals upward via transpiration pull and root pressure.
- **Phloem (សរសៃរនាម):** Translocates organic sucrose from source leaves to sink storage organs via pressure-flow mechanics.
