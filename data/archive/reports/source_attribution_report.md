## 2. Document Verification Standards

Every entry in the ScienceKH inventory has been verified according to the following 5 criteria:

1. **Ministerial Authority Check:**
   Does the document bear the official header of the Kingdom of Cambodia (`ព្រះរាជាណាចក្រកម្ពុជា ជាតិ សាសនា ព្រះមហាក្សត្រ`) and the Ministry seal?
2. **Session Date Integrity:**
   Does the stated exam date correspond to the actual gazetted date of the national examination (e.g. October 8–9, 2024; November 6–7, 2023)?
3. **Temporal Distinction:**
   Examination year is kept distinct from publication year and upload year. A 2024 reprint of a 2017 exam is cataloged as `examYear: 2017` and `publicationYear: 2024`.
4. **Compilation Disambiguation:**
   Multi-year books covering spans (e.g. 2014–2020) are explicitly cataloged as `multi_year_compilation` and never broken into false single-year records unless the individual exam sheet is verified intact.
5. **No Speculative Data:**
   Years 2025 and 2026 are designated as `PLANNED` and contain no fabricated mock records.
