# ScienceKH — Missing Examination Papers & Digitization Gap Report (2012–2026)

**Purpose:** Transparent accounting of gaps in historical examination coverage to prevent fabricated or speculative data entry.

---

## 1. Scope of Gaps

In accordance with strict archival standards, missing examination papers are explicitly identified below. No fictional or practice questions have been used to fill these gaps.

---

## 2. Gap Breakdown by Category & Subject

### A. Bac II National Examination — Science Stream
* **Earth and Environmental Science (ផែនដី និងបរិស្ថានវិទ្យា):**
  * *Missing Years:* 2012, 2013, 2014, 2015, 2016, 2017, 2018, 2019, 2021, 2022, 2023.
  * *Notes:* In most examination sessions, Earth Science was an elective or minor subject where digitized standalone PDFs were rarely retained independently of bound compilations.
* **Physics & Chemistry (រូបវិទ្យា និងគីមីវិទ្យា):**
  * *Single Paper Missing:* 2012, 2013, 2016, 2017, 2019, 2021, 2022 (individual PDFs).
  * *Note on Compilations:* Many of these are bound inside `BACII-COMPILATION-2014-2017-SCI` and will be extracted during secondary digitization.
* **English & French (ភាសាបរទេស):**
  * *Missing Years:* All historical years except 2024.
  * *Notes:* Foreign language tests are classified as qualifying pass/fail criteria in earlier years.

### B. Bac II National Examination — Social Science Stream
* **History, Geography, Moral-Civics (ប្រវត្តិវិទ្យា, ភូមិវិទ្យា, សីលធម៌-ពលរដ្ឋ):**
  * *Missing Individual Years:* 2012, 2013, 2015, 2016, 2018, 2019, 2021, 2022, 2023.
  * *Status:* MoEYS and educational blogs historically prioritized Science Stream STEM subjects for digital preservation. Social science papers exist primarily in printed paper format in provincial education offices and need direct scanning.
  * *Compilation Coverage:* Years 2014–2017 are partially preserved in the MoEYS Social Science Compilation book.

### C. National Outstanding Students Examination (ប្រឡងសិស្សពូកែ)
* **Grade 9 Outstanding Students (ថ្នាក់ទី៩):**
  * *Available:* 2024 (Math), 2022 (Math).
  * *Missing Years:* 2012, 2013, 2014, 2015, 2016, 2017, 2018, 2019, 2020, 2021, 2023 (Math, Physics, Khmer).
* **Grade 12 Outstanding Students (ថ្នាក់ទី១២):**
  * *Available:* 2024 (Math), 2023 (Math, Khmer), 2019 (Math), 2018 (Math), 2013 (Math).
  * *Missing Years:* 2012, 2014, 2015, 2016, 2017, 2020, 2021, 2022 (Physics, Khmer).

## 4. Remediation & Retrieval Roadmap

1. **MoEYS Telegram News Archive Mining:**
   Systematic date filtering on `t.me/moeysnews` during the months of August (2014–2019) and November/December (2021–2023) to capture raw announcement photos and PDF releases.
2. **PTEC Library Direct Partnership:**
   The Phnom Penh Teacher Education College (`library.ptec.edu.kh`) retains bound archival copies of past exams used for teacher trainee pedagogical training.
3. **Provincial Department Inquiries:**
   Targeting Kandal, Siem Reap, Battambang, and Kampong Cham provincial departments that hosted the National Outstanding Students examinations.
